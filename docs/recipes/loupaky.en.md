# Loupáky (sweet poppy seed buns)

> Sweet yeasted buns topped with poppy seeds — a fragrant Czech breakfast classic.

**Servings:** 6 buns · **Prep:** 30 min + 1 h 15 min rising · **Bake:** 20 min

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 150 ml milk (lukewarm, 30–40 °C)
- 15 g fresh yeast
- 315 g plain flour
- 75 g butter (softened)
- 37 g cane sugar (≈ 2 tbsp)
- 2 tsp vanilla sugar
- 1 egg yolk
- pinch of salt

### Topping

- 1 egg white
- 2 tbsp poppy seeds

## Step by step

1. **Starter.** Pour the milk into a bowl, crumble in the yeast and add one spoonful of sugar (from the measured amount). Stir, cover with a tea towel and let it foam up for about 10 minutes.
2. **Dough.** In a large bowl (or stand mixer with a hook) combine the flour, the rest of the cane sugar, vanilla sugar and salt. Add the butter and the yolk, pour in the starter and knead into a smooth, elastic dough (~8–10 minutes in a mixer, longer by hand).
3. **First rise.** Cover and let rise in a warm place until doubled in size (~1 hour).
4. **Portioning.** Tip onto a floured surface and divide into 6 equal pieces. Shape each into a ball and let rest under a tea towel for 10 minutes.
5. **Rolling.** Roll each ball into a teardrop shape and roll up from the wide end to the tip. Place on a tray lined with baking paper.
6. **Wash and topping.** Beat the egg white with a fork, brush the buns and sprinkle with poppy seeds.
7. **Second rise.** Let them rise on the tray for another 15 minutes. Meanwhile preheat the oven to 180 °C.
8. **Bake.** Bake at 180 °C for about 20 minutes, until golden.
9. **Cool.** Transfer to a wire rack and let cool.

!!! note "Notes"
    **Storage:** wrap in a tea towel and keep in a bread bin — not plastic.

    **Dry yeast:** 15 g fresh ≈ 5 g instant (Saf-instant). Mix dry yeast straight into the flour, no starter needed.
