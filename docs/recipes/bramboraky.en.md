# Bramboráky (Czech potato pancakes)

> Crispy fried potato pancakes with garlic and marjoram — a Czech classic.

**Servings:** 4 · **Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 20 min

## Ingredients

- 1 kg potatoes
- 2 eggs
- 4 cloves of garlic
- 3 tbsp plain flour
- 1 tsp dried marjoram
- 1 tsp caraway seeds
- salt, pepper
- oil or lard for frying

## Step by step

1. Peel the potatoes and grate them finely. Squeeze out as much liquid as you can.
2. Add eggs, crushed garlic, flour, marjoram, caraway, salt and pepper. Mix into a thick batter.
3. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
4. Spoon the batter into the pan and flatten into thin pancakes.
5. Fry 3–4 minutes per side until deep golden brown and crispy.
6. Drain on paper towels and serve immediately.

!!! tip
    Serve with sauerkraut, pickles, or just on their own with a glass of cold beer.
