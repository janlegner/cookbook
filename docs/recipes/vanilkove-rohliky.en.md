# Vanilla crescents (Vanilkové rohlíky)

> Buttery, nutty crescents rolled in vanilla sugar — a Czech Christmas classic.

**Servings:** ~50 cookies · **Prep:** 20 min + 1 h chilling · **Bake:** 12 min

## Ingredients

### Dough

- 280 g plain flour
- 100 g powdered sugar
- 100 g ground almonds (or walnuts)
- 210 g cold butter
- 1 egg yolk
- pinch of salt
- 1 vanilla pod (seeds scraped) or 1 sachet of vanilla sugar

### Coating

- 100 g powdered sugar
- 2 sachets of vanilla sugar

## Step by step

1. Mix flour, powdered sugar, ground almonds, salt and vanilla in a bowl.
2. Add the cold butter cut into cubes and the egg yolk. Quickly knead into a smooth dough — don't overwork it or the butter will warm up.
3. Wrap in cling film and chill for at least 1 hour.
4. Pinch off small pieces, roll into thin logs and shape into crescents. Place on a lined baking tray.
5. Bake in a preheated oven at 180 °C for about 10–12 minutes, until just lightly golden. They should not brown.
6. Mix the powdered sugar with the vanilla sugar in a bowl.
7. While still warm, gently roll the crescents in the sugar mix and let them cool.

!!! tip
    Store in a tin with a slice of apple — after a few days they turn beautifully soft.
