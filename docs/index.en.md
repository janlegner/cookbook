# Cookbook

Welcome to my personal cookbook. Browse the [recipes](recipes/index.md) section to find something to cook.

## How recipes are organized

Every recipe follows the same simple structure:

1. **Ingredients** — what you need.
2. **Picture** *(optional)* — what the result should look like.
3. **Step by step** — how to make it.

## Languages

This cookbook is available in English and Czech. Use the language switcher in the top-right corner.
